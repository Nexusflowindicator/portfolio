FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Connect your calendar in the browser and Mintmeet handles the rest.

Which meeting tools are supported?

Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, plus uploaded audio and video files.

Who can see my meeting notes?

Only the people you share them with. Admins on the Business plan can set company-wide rules.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month to month, and you can export all your notes at any time.
